Get back to nature and lose yourself.

Bill's Boathouse is a tiny house moored on the Murray River at White Sands, for two. About an hour from Adelaide.

When the sun decides to set, light the fire or soak in a steaming outdoor clawfoot bath.

After dark the pelicans fish around the festoon lights.

A wrap-around deck, straight onto the river

Grab a deck chair or the hammock and watch the swans and pelicans come by. Fish off the edge, paddle out in a kayak, then light the fire when the sun goes.

Kayak all day

Two kayaks wait at the deck with life jackets. The river is wide and flat here, with willows along the bank, and the swans and pelicans come by every day.

In summer, on long weekends and in school holidays the river gets busier, with skiers out on the water. The rest of the time, guests mostly write about how quiet it is.

“we loved the kayaks and wildlife that would come up and see us.”Lauren, October 2026, on Airbnb

Inside: one room for two

  • Sleeps 2
  • Queen bed
  • Pets welcome
  • Kitchen
  • Rain shower
  • Heating and cooling

The bedroom has a built-in wardrobe, a queen bed in Sheridan linen and warm American oak driftwood floors.

The kitchen is small but complete: two burners, a toaster oven, a bar fridge and a little dishwasher. There's a plunger for coffee.

Shop in Murray Bridge on the way in. Guests find it hard to leave once they arrive.

Sunrise, sunset, and the mist in between

River mist in the morning, sunsets over the water, stars at night, all from the same deck.

Built in 2016 from mostly recycled materials

Chris Clarke, an award-winning builder, built the boathouse off site, away from the river, to keep its impact low, with low-energy lights, louvre windows for the breeze, double glazing and insulated floors, walls and ceilings.

  • South Australian Tourism Awards, finalist 2024
  • Best Riverside Getaway, APAC Insider Australian Enterprise Awards 2023, 2024 and 2025
  • Airbnb Host Awards, Best Unique Stay, finalist
  • Australian Ecotourism Certified, and a Quality and Sustainable Tourism Accredited Business

Riverglen Marina, White Sands

The boathouse is moored for good at the Riverglen Marina Reserve, south-east of Adelaide, on Ngarrindjeri country. There are other houseboats in the marina, so you'll have neighbours on the water.

  • Murray Bridgeabout 15 min
  • Hahndorfunder an hour
  • Adelaideabout an hour

Pets are welcome on board. The water is right at the deck edge, so it isn't suited to small children.
